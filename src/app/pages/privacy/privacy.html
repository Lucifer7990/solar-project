Aussie Premium Solar Pty Ltd ("Aussie Premium Solar", "we", "us" or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy and managing your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store and protect your personal information when you interact with us, our website, our employees, contractors or authorised representatives.

1

Collection of Personal Information

We may collect personal information directly from you when you request a quotation; contact us by phone, email, SMS or through our website; submit an online enquiry or callback request; purchase our products or services; request finance information; register for promotions or newsletters; participate in surveys or competitions; communicate with our customer support team; or otherwise interact with our business.

The personal information we collect may include full name; residential or installation address; postal address; email address; telephone number; date of birth (where required); electricity retailer and distributor details; electricity account information; property information relevant to your installation; payment information; identification documents where required for rebates or finance; and any other information reasonably required to provide our products and services.

We may also collect technical information when you visit our website, including IP address; browser type; operating system; device information; pages visited; referring website; cookies and similar technologies.

2

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve user experience, monitor website performance and deliver relevant advertising.

Cookies help us remember your preferences; analyse website traffic; improve website functionality; measure advertising effectiveness; and provide remarketing advertisements.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some website features may not function correctly.

We may use services including Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta (Facebook) Pixel, Microsoft Advertising, and other marketing and analytics platforms.

We may use your personal information to provide quotations; design and install solar and battery systems; arrange inspections; process orders and payments; administer government rebate applications; obtain DNSP approvals; coordinate installations with subcontractors; provide warranty support; communicate with you regarding your project; respond to enquiries; improve our products and services; conduct customer satisfaction surveys; send promotional offers and marketing communications; comply with legal and regulatory obligations; and detect and prevent fraud.

We may disclose your personal information where reasonably necessary to provide our services, including to our employees; authorised subcontractors; CEC accredited installers; electricians; wholesalers and equipment suppliers; Distribution Network Service Providers (DNSPs); electricity retailers; government departments administering rebates; finance providers (where requested); payment processors; IT service providers; cloud storage providers; CRM providers; call centres; marketing providers; professional advisers; insurers; legal advisers; regulators; and any person where required or authorised by law.

Where this occurs, we take reasonable steps to ensure they comply with the Australian Privacy Principles or equivalent privacy protections.
