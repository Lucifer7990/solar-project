Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the manner in which Aussie Premium Solar Pty Ltd (ACN 698 723 027) (“we”, “us” or “our”) manages personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) (the “Privacy Law”). This Privacy Policy applies to all products and services offered by Aussie Premium Solar and explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect personal information.

1

Collecting your personal information

We may employ various ways in order to collect your personal information, including, but not limited to, the time of your visit to the website, request for a quote, request for a call back, your registration on the site, placement an order, subscription to a newsletter, filling out of any forms, and in connection with any activity, service, features, or resources made available by us on and through the website.

This personal information shall typically consist of your name, email address, and contact number. However, you may also visit our website anonymously. Additionally, we may collect non-personal information about you when you interact with the website. Such non-personal information includes, but is not limited to, your browser name, the type of machine you are using to access the website, and any other technical information about your means of connecting such as your operating system, internet service provides, and the like.

Our website may make use of “cookies” to enhance your browsing experience. Said cookies are placed by the web browser on its hard drive in order to keep a record of your activities and to track information about you. You may choose to change your browser settings in order to refuse cookies, or to notify you when cookies are being sent. If you do so, you may observe that some portions of the website do not work properly. Users with remarketing cookies may be targeted across advertising networks to receive relevant advertisements. You are at liberty to opt out of this ad serving through the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) opt-out page www.networkadvertising.org, or you may opt out of the Google Remarketing Program through the Google Ad Preference Manager: www.networkadvertising.org. You may also use the Google Analytics Opt–Out Browser Add–on tool to quit the Google Remarketing Program.

2

Using your personal information

We may use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To deliver the products and services you have requested.
  • To notify you regarding our offers or deals.
  • To enhance our customer service and experience – your information may enable us to respond to your customer service requests in a more efficient manner.
  • To understand and analyse how users like you, as a group, use the services and resources available on our website.
  • To improve our products and services.
  • To run a promotion, contest, host a survey, or any other website feature.
  • To provide you with information you have agreed to receive, or information which we think will be of interest to you.
  • To send emails, text messages or newsletters.
3

Disclosing your personal information

You agree and consent to us sharing your personal information with Aussie Premium Solar subsidiaries. We use third party service providers (such as our call-centre) to help us operate our business and this website or administer activities on our behalf. We will take reasonable steps to require the overseas recipient to comply with Australian Privacy Principles unless the recipient is subject to laws that would protect your information in a substantially similar way to the Australian Privacy Principles. By providing us with your personal information, you consent to the us communicating said information to third–party service providers. We shall disclose your personal information only if such disclosure is required by or authorised under an Australian law or a court / tribunal order.

4

Securing information

We employ appropriate and relevant data collection, storage, processing practices, and safety measures to ensure protection against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction of your personal information, username, password, transaction information, and any other data stores on our website.

5

Accessing your information

You can access your information by sending us a request in writing. We shall provide you with the requested information, provided such information does not violate another person’s privacy. Upon your requesting access, you may be required to provide us with some form identification (such as a driver’s licence) in order to verify that you are the person to whom the information relates. Under certain circumstances, you may be asked to pay a fee to cover the cost of providing access to such information. If you wish to know what personal information we have about you, please feel free to write to our Privacy Officer at info@aussiepremiumsolar.com.au.

6

Correcting your information

Should you believe that any information we hold about you is incorrect or outdated, or if you have concerns about how we are handling your personal information, please contact our Privacy Officer at info@aussiepremiumsolar.com.au and we will try to address your concerns. In case you wish to have your personal information deleted from our records, please write to our Privacy Officer at info@aussiepremiumsolar.com.au and we will ensure that it is deleted.

7

Complaints

Should you have any issues or complaints, including any complaints that we have failed to comply with the Australian Privacy Principles, feel free to address our Privacy Officer at info@aussiepremiumsolar.com.au regarding such concerns.

8

Unsubscribe

If you wish to unsubscribe or opt out of any communication with us, please write to our Privacy Officer at info@aussiepremiumsolar.com.au.

9

Updates to privacy policy

We may make certain changes to our Privacy Policy in order to ensure that it is in accordance with the Privacy Law.
